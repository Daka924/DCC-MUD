# The crawler and death

## What lasts — Decided

Your crawler is like an MMO character: you keep the same character across sessions. But death is permanent... ish.
The game is full of weird classes, abilities, races and other quirky things that could save you from death or
bring you back from it.

## When your crawler dies — Decided

1. **If something you have can bring you back, you return as yourself.** That can be a race, a class, or an
   ability you were granted or earned.
2. **Otherwise your crawler is gone.** You pick what to play next:
   - **A new crawler**, chosen from a list of 3 whacky pre-made entries; or
   - **Take over one of your NPC party members** who is still alive.
3. **Where and how strong:** you always come back on the **current floor**, at the **average level of the
   players** in the dungeon.

## Proposed

| Rule | Proposal |
| --- | --- |
| The 3 whacky entries | Each has a name, a past life, a starting kit and one odd quirk; generated from the same parts as character creation, so no two lists are the same |
| A new crawler after floor 3 | Arrives with a race and class already picked by the show, from the usual 10 and 10 offers, since they missed the choice |
| Taking over an NPC | You keep the NPC's class, gear and personality quirks; the narrator never lets you forget it |
| What the dead leave behind | A grave on the floor with their gear in it; anyone can loot it, and the narrator comments on who does |
