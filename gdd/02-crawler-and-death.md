# The crawler and death

## What lasts — Decided

Your crawler is like an MMO character: you keep the same character across sessions. But death is permanent... ish.
The game is full of weird classes, abilities, races and other quirky things that could save you from death or
bring you back from it.

## Death — Proposed

| Rule | Proposal |
| --- | --- |
| Default outcome | Death is permanent unless something you own says otherwise |
| Ways back (examples) | A race that revives once, a class ability that cheats a killing blow, a consumable, a friend's resurrection ability, a god's favour |
| Floors 1 and 2 | Open: should there be any free respawns while players learn? |

## Open

- What happens to a player whose crawler is dead for good: start a new crawler, and where (floor 1, or the floor
  the group is on)?
- Does a dead crawler leave anything behind (loot, a ghost, a grave other players can visit)?
