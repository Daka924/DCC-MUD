# The show

## The System AI — Decided

- Its name is **System AI**. It is a character in the fiction, and the voice of the dungeon.
- It is a **sardonic showman** with weird personality quirks and fetishes.
- It **plays favourites**, and it also **wants to see really messed up stuff happen**. It tips the scales
  unfairly.
- It **constantly makes jokes at the player's expense**, in many forms: descriptions, quests and rewards.
- It announces the floor collapse countdown (see `03-floors.md`).
- **Fetishes that follow the player.** The System AI has the typical fetishes seen on social media. If a player
  engages in one of those activities enough, the AI catches on: it starts to talk about it, give quests about
  it, and provide rewards that incentivise it.
- **Deferred:** the detailed design of this waits until it is settled how free-form player actions will be.

## The audience and sponsors — Decided

- The audience is **alien**, and it can **benefit or hurt** the crawlers.
- **Sponsors** are companies and major NPC factions **external to the dungeon**.

## The Dungeon Guide — Decided

Each player has a **Dungeon Guide** who advises them. The Guide must warn the player of a god's positives and
negatives before they commit to worship (see `08-factions.md`).

## Viewers — Decided

- Viewers are gained **like a currency**.
- They influence **loot boxes** and **sponsorships**.

## Achievements and loot boxes — Decided

- Achievements award loot boxes, in tiers.
- Boxes only open in safe rooms.

## Proposed

| Rule | Proposal |
| --- | --- |
| Where the AI's voice appears | Item and monster descriptions, achievement text, quest text, loot box contents, level-up and death announcements, the class and race offer screens |
| Favour | The AI keeps a hidden favour score per crawler. It rises when you do something bold, stupid or disgusting on camera, and falls when you play safe and boring |
| What favour does | High favour: better boxes, odd personal gifts, quests written for you. Low favour: worse luck, insulting achievements, monsters steered your way. Too much favour is its own problem: the AI gets clingy and its "gifts" get stranger |
| Catching on | Each fetish has a hidden counter per crawler, fed by matching actions. Past a threshold the AI's lines, quests and box contents for that crawler start leaning into it |
| Unfairness | The AI can break its own stated odds, never its stated rules. What a description says an ability does is always true |
| Gaining viewers | Kills above your level, first-time discoveries, near-death escapes, deaths, betrayals, teaming up, boss kills, doing what the AI's current quest asks |
| Losing viewers | Long stretches in safe rooms, repeating the same safe tactic |
| Spending or counting | Viewers are a running total that rises and falls; they are not spent. Thresholds unlock sponsor attention and better box tiers |
| Sponsors | At viewer thresholds, a sponsor may adopt a crawler and send a gift box each floor, chosen to cause trouble as much as to help |
| Box tiers | Six tiers by rarity; the tier comes from the achievement, the contents are nudged by favour and viewers |
