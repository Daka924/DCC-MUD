# Factions and reputation

## Decided

- Players earn reputation with **any faction they quest with and complete quests for**.
- Reputation **unlocks that faction's race as a choice** at the later race selection.
- Players can **work for multiple factions**.
- Players **lose standing** with a faction when they take actions that directly hurt that faction's numbers, such
  as killing its members.
- Anything in the dungeon can be **neutral, aggressive or friendly** to a given player.

## Proposed

| Rule | Proposal |
| --- | --- |
| Standing levels | Hated, Disliked, Neutral, Liked, Trusted, One of Us |
| Race unlock | Reaching Trusted adds that faction's race to your floor 3 offers |
| Losing standing | Each kill of a member costs standing; killing a named member or leader costs a lot. Helping a rival in a contested zone costs a little |
| How they treat you | Hated: attack on sight. Disliked: no trade, no quests. Liked and above: quests, trade, recipes, safe passage |
| Playing both sides | Allowed until a contested attraction's final quest, which forces a choice or a betrayal |
