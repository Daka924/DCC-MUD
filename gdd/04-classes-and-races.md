# Classes and races

## When you get them — Decided

- You must reach **floor 3** to pick a class and a race.
- You choose from **10 race options and 10 class options**, each list influenced by what you have done in the
  dungeon.
- Before the choice, the show presents your file:
  - your past (the life you had before the dungeon),
  - the items and abilities you used,
  - the race factions you built up reputation with,
  - and which options would be strong with your class picks.
- Classes have a **natural progression**. As you descend, class selection is presented again and you can evolve
  into a stronger class of the next tier, with stronger abilities and new specialised tactics, spells and ideas.
- For now only the floor 3 choice needs designing.

## What the reference says about later tiers — for information

The source material (record ids FL01, FL04, CR04, CL022, CL037, CL038):

- The class is chosen on floor 3. The choice is permanent and on a timer, and a random option is taken if the
  player does not answer.
- Some classes **upgrade into a renamed, stronger form** partway through, keeping what came before (an ice
  caster becomes an ice-and-earth caster, then an ice, earth and wind caster).
- A permanent **specialisation is chosen on arriving at floor 6**, and keeps all earlier upgrades.

**Proposed:** tier 1 at floor 3, an upgrade to tier 2 offered on floor 4 or 5 when you meet that class's
condition, and tier 3 (the specialisation) on arriving at floor 6.

## The offer list of 10 — Proposed

| Slots | Where they come from |
| --- | --- |
| 7 | The classes whose *offered when* conditions the player met most strongly |
| 2 | Generated classes built from the player's tags (past life, items, abilities, factions, choices) |
| 1 | A plain classic |

Every slot is a legitimate class; there is no joke slot.

Timer: 10 minutes. If the player does not choose, one is picked at random.

## Races

### Decided

- 10 race options at floor 3, influenced by the race factions the player built reputation with.
- A race gives: **a body style (sometimes)**, **stats**, **a smattering of racial abilities**, and **at least one
  weakness**.
- Races tend towards **fantasy ideas**: think of the races from RPG-style video games.
- **Primal is a race.** In the source material (record ids RA002, CR07) it works like this: you keep your existing
  body and whatever came with it, take **-1 to every base stat**, and in exchange can **train skills past the
  normal cap** (to 20 where others stop at 15). It is a long-term growth choice, not an immediate power boost.

### Proposed

| Rule | Proposal |
| --- | --- |
| Primal in our game | Always one of the 10 offers. Keep your body, -1 to all stats, skill and ability cap raised from 15 to 20 |
| Race offers | Up to 6 from factions you have reputation with on floors 1 and 2, the rest chosen to pair well with your class offers, plus Primal |
| Order of choosing | Race first, then class, so class offers can react to the race |
| Racial abilities | 2 to 3 each: usually one passive, one active, one that only matters in certain places (water, dark, crowds) |
