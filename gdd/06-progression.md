# Progression

## Character creation — Decided

A new crawler chooses a **name**, a **look** and a **past job**, and enters the dungeon with **the few things
they had on them**.

## Floors 1 and 2: choose your own destiny — Decided

Before they have a class, crawlers still have abilities and gear. What they have depends on:

- what they brought into the dungeon,
- what they find,
- and how they use it.

What grows on floors 1 and 2:

- **Skills level up** with use.
- Players can **find magical items and abilities**.
- Players **gain experience and levels**. Each level they **choose from a broad set of skills and abilities**, and
  **assign stat points**.
- Players can learn magic, craft explosives, fashion weapons and armour, adopt pets, and more.

Player agency decides what they will have available later: everything above feeds the class and race offers on
floor 3.

## Open

- How crafting works (who can craft, what it needs).
- How pets are found and adopted.
