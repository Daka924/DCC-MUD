# Vision

## Pitch — Decided

> You're a nobody dropped into a televised death dungeon; you survive floor by floor, turn into something absurd
> and powerful, and play to the crowd.

## What kind of game — Decided

A Diablo / Path of Exile style pixel-art MUD. You have been dropped into the dungeon. To survive and finish each
floor you kill enemies, defeat bosses, collect loot, and grow your skills and abilities.

Classes come from a wacky mix of weird real-life ideas and typical fantasy ideas, and which ones you are offered is
inspired by the decisions you make as you play.

The three things the game asks of a player:

1. **Make brash decisions.**
2. **Gather some friends.**
3. **Impress the audience.**

## Fixed points from earlier conversations — Decided

| Topic | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| View | Diablo-style isometric pixel art |
| Engine | Godot 4 |
| Story engine | No paid or hosted AI. Rules over data; a free local model is acceptable |
| Combat | Built around spells and abilities: offensive, defensive and supportive. Throwable items may exist but are not the core |
| Centrepiece | A huge range of weird classes, offered after surviving the first two floors, shaped by the player's background and choices |
| IP | Original work. Keep the genre, mechanics and tone; use Daka's own world, names and characters |
| Setting | Real places in and around New Jersey, using their real names, twisted into dungeon floors (see `03-floors.md`) |
| Audience | A small community of 2 to 20 players. The repository is private |
