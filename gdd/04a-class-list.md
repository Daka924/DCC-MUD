# Class list — Proposed

**Target (Decided): close to 150 classes.** This file holds the first 28.

Every class here is a first draft written to the ground rules in `04-classes-and-races.md`. Strike any you dislike,
rename freely, and add your own. Each class lists three **signature abilities** it starts with. They are not the
limit: a crawler keeps everything learned on floors 1 and 2 and can keep unlocking more (see
`05-abilities-and-combat.md`). The headings below say where a class is strongest, not what it is locked into.

Your three examples from the source material (the riot-cop explosives class, the pasta mage and the monster truck
driver) are tone references only and are not in the list, because the game uses original classes.

## Offensive leaning

**Ice Cream Truck Juggernaut** — *the job's vehicle becomes your body*
- The jingle plays before you arrive. Monsters that hear it panic.
- Signature abilities: **Brain Freeze**: charge in a line, knocking down and chilling everything hit. **Out of Order**: the
  shutter slams, halving damage taken. **Sprinkles**: allies near you get a burst of speed.
- Drawback: you cannot sneak; the jingle never stops.
- Offered when: you killed a lot by running into things, or used cold abilities often.
- Tier 2: **Soft-Serve Siege Engine**.

**Dirty Water Pyromancer** — *street hot dog cart, but fire magic*
- Signature abilities: **Relish Fire**: a cone of burning condiment. **Umbrella Stand**: a heat shield that burns anything that
  hits it. **Lunch Rush**: allies who eat from your cart heal and hit harder for a while.
- Drawback: water terrain halves your power.
- Offered when: you used fire a lot, or ate a lot of found food.
- Tier 2: **Food Truck Inferno Lord**.

**Overnight Stocker Assassin** — *the aisle at 3 AM is your hunting ground*
- Signature abilities: **Box Cutter Flurry**: fast strikes, much stronger from behind. **Clean-Up on Aisle Four**: vanish in a
  slick that trips pursuers. **Restock**: refill an ally's consumables and potions.
- Drawback: weaker in daylight and open outdoor areas.
- Offered when: you killed from stealth often, or looted every shelf on floor 1.
- Tier 2: **Graveyard Shift Shade**.

**Gym Bro Berserker** — *the pump is a real buff*
- Signature abilities: **Personal Record**: grab an enemy and throw it into others. **The Pump**: take less damage and grow
  stronger the longer the fight lasts. **Spot Me**: an ally near you cannot drop below 1 health for a few seconds.
- Drawback: Intelligence capped low; spell scrolls make you nervous.
- Offered when: you put most stat points into Strength, or fought with bare hands a lot.
- Tier 2: **Olympian of the Iron**.

**Asphaltomancer** — *road crew magic*
- Signature abilities: **Pothole**: the floor opens under a group of enemies. **Fresh Blacktop**: a patch of hot tar around you that
  roots and burns. **Detour**: redirect monsters away from an area for a while.
- Drawback: useless on water, ice and upper floors of buildings.
- Offered when: you used earth or terrain abilities, or spent a long time on the roads of floor 2.
- Tier 2: **Turnpike Archmage**.

**Auctioneer Hexer** — *fast talk as a curse*
- Signature abilities: **Going Once, Going Twice**: a stacking curse; the third call kills weak enemies outright. **Sold!**: claim
  the last enemy you hit and step away from danger. **Bidding War**: allies' damage grows while they compete for
  the most hits.
- Drawback: abilities get weaker if you stay silent (do not use one) for too long.
- Offered when: you sold or traded a lot, or chatted a lot.
- Tier 2: **Auction House Overlord**.

## Defensive leaning

**Toll Booth Warden** — *the gatekeeper knight of the Turnpike*
- Signature abilities: **Exact Change**: marks an enemy; it takes more damage every time it crosses your line. **Booth**: wall
  yourself into a box that blocks everything for a few seconds. **Fast Lane**: a lane where allies move faster and
  enemies move slower.
- Drawback: much weaker while moving.
- Offered when: you held doorways and chokepoints, or used defensive abilities more than any other kind.
- Tier 2: **Turnpike Authority**.

**Bouncer Golem** — *the door is the job*
- Signature abilities: **Not Tonight**: a shove that sends enemies flying. **Velvet Rope**: a barrier enemies cannot cross.
  **VIP**: an ally in your aura takes part of their damage onto you instead.
- Drawback: slow; cannot use stealth.
- Offered when: you took a lot of hits and survived, or protected others.
- Tier 2: **Door God**.

**Personal Injury Paladin** — *the billboard lawyer as a holy warrior*
- Signature abilities: **Objection!**: interrupt an enemy's attack and stun it. **Whiplash**: damage you take is stored as a
  settlement. **Class Action**: the party shares damage evenly; then you pay out the settlement as a burst.
- Drawback: you get nothing from healing done to you while a settlement is pending.
- Offered when: you were hit a lot by large monsters, or had many close calls.
- Tier 2: **Litigator Saint**.

**Crossing Guard Sentinel** — *the stop sign is a shield*
- Signature abilities: **STOP**: freeze every enemy in a line. **Reflective Vest**: reflect the next ranged attack. **Safe
  Crossing**: allies in a zone cannot be hit while moving through it.
- Drawback: all your abilities need a straight line.
- Offered when: you protected companions, or stood still while others ran.
- Tier 2: **Warden of the Crossroads**.

**Customer Service Martyr** — *absorbing abuse is a superpower*
- Signature abilities: **Escalate to Manager**: summon a huge angry spirit that fights for a short while. **Stay on Script**: immune
  to taunts, fear and charm. **Hold Music**: everything near you, friend or foe, slows down; allies heal during it.
- Drawback: damage you take is not reduced by armour.
- Offered when: you were hit a lot and never fled, or argued with NPCs.
- Tier 2: **Supervisor of Suffering**.

**Mattress Salesman Dreamweaver** — *the sale ends tonight, and so do you*
- Signature abilities: **Going Out of Business**: enemies in an area fall asleep; the next hit wakes them and deals extra damage.
  **Memory Foam**: absorb the next few hits completely. **Sleep Number**: allies resting near you heal much
  faster, even outside safe rooms.
- Drawback: you are drowsy; you act slower after using an ability.
- Offered when: you spent a long time in safe rooms, or used sleep and slow effects.
- Tier 2: **Lord of the Showroom Floor**.

## Supportive leaning

**Diner Oracle** — *the all-night Jersey diner waitress who sees your future*
- Signature abilities: **Hot Coffee**: a cone of scalding coffee. **Be Right With You**: vanish for a moment. **Bottomless
  Refill**: an ally heals over time and their next ability is free.
- Drawback: your abilities cost more if you have not eaten recently.
- Offered when: you ate a lot, healed others, or spent time with floor 1's food court factions.
- Tier 2: **Short-Order Sibyl**.

**Funeral Director Necromancer** — *death care, but the dead get back up*
- Signature abilities: **Open Casket**: raise a fresh corpse to fight for you for a while. **Embalm**: turn your skin to stone
  briefly. **Pallbearers**: carry a downed ally to safety and bring them back with some health.
- Drawback: living NPCs distrust you; shop prices are higher.
- Offered when: you looted corpses a lot, or an ally died near you.
- Tier 2: **Undertaker of the Deep**.

**Lifeguard Templar** — *the Shore's watcher*
- Signature abilities: **Whistle**: a blast that stuns everything in front of you. **Riptide**: pull yourself out of danger in a
  rush of water. **Rescue Tube**: yank an ally to your side and shield them.
- Drawback: weaker away from water.
- Offered when: you swam or fought in water a lot, or saved allies on low health.
- Tier 2: **Tidewarden**.

**Tattoo Artist Runecarver** — *buffs are ink*
- Signature abilities: **Flash Sheet**: draw a rune on the floor that bursts when stepped on. **Second Skin**: armour drawn on
  yourself. **Fresh Ink**: give an ally a lasting buff tattoo; one per ally.
- Drawback: tattoos fade when you die, on everyone.
- Offered when: you used many different buff items, or got many permanent marks or curses.
- Tier 2: **Master of the Living Ink**.

**Podcast Host Seer** — *you know everything because you talk about everything*
- Signature abilities: **Hot Take**: a cone of damage that grows with the number of enemies. **Ad Read**: everything stops
  attacking you while you read the sponsor message. **Deep Dive**: reveal the map around you and every enemy's
  weakness to the party.
- Drawback: you must talk; chat silence weakens you.
- Offered when: you chatted a lot, or found maps and secrets.
- Tier 2: **Network Prophet**.

**Wedding Planner Summoner** — *the bridal party is an army*
- Signature abilities: **Bridal Party**: summon a group of minions. **Seating Chart**: swap places with any of your minions.
  **Timeline**: allies' cooldowns run faster while the schedule holds; it breaks if you take a big hit.
- Drawback: minions disappear if you go too long without a fight.
- Offered when: you recruited many companions, or led a party.
- Tier 2: **Matrimonial Archon**.

## Control and trickery

**HOA Inquisitor** — *bylaws as divine law*
- Signature abilities: **Violation Notice**: a stacking mark; three marks and the target is fined (big damage). **Bylaws**: in a zone
  around you, enemies cannot use abilities for a few seconds. **Neighborhood Watch**: the party shares vision.
- Drawback: social NPCs dislike you; reputation gains are halved.
- Offered when: you fought factions over territory, or kept everything clean and orderly.
- Tier 2: **Grand Covenant Enforcer**.

**DMV Clerk Chronomancer** — *time magic from the slowest place on earth*
- Signature abilities: **Take a Number**: delay an enemy's next action by several seconds. **Window Closed**: freeze yourself in
  time, untouchable. **Expedited**: reset an ally's cooldowns.
- Drawback: your own cooldowns are longer than anyone's.
- Offered when: you waited a lot (idle time, safe rooms), or used slow effects.
- Tier 2: **Bureau of Eternity**.

**Boardwalk Carny Trickster** — *every game is rigged*
- Signature abilities: **Ring Toss**: rings that bind whoever they land on. **Funhouse Mirror**: create copies of yourself.
  **Winner Every Time**: the party's loot luck rises for a while.
- Drawback: NPC merchants will not trade with you.
- Offered when: you gambled with loot boxes, or tricked enemies.
- Tier 2: **Ringmaster of the Last Pier**.

**Union Steward Warlock** — *a pact with the local*
- Signature abilities: **Grievance**: a curse that grows each time the target hits an ally. **Work Stoppage**: enemies in an area
  freeze for a moment. **Solidarity**: the party shares one health pool for a few seconds.
- Drawback: you cannot act alone well; abilities are weaker with no ally nearby.
- Offered when: you teamed up with real players a lot, or joined a faction.
- Tier 2: **Archon of the Brotherhood**.

**Repo Knight** — *collections are a crusade*
- Signature abilities: **Repossess**: steal an enemy's buff and use it. **Lien**: delay damage you take and pay it off slowly.
  **Grace Period**: an ally who would die is held at 1 health for a few seconds instead.
- Drawback: you carry debt; part of your gold income is always taken.
- Offered when: you stole items or picked up everything in sight.
- Tier 2: **Collections Crusader**.

## Audience and performance

**Influencer Lich** — *the phone is the phylactery*
- Signature abilities: **Ratio'd**: damage that grows with your viewer count. **Story Mode**: once per floor, if your viewers are
  high enough, you come back from death at your phone. **Collab**: an ally gains a share of your viewers' favour.
- Drawback: when viewers drop, your power drops with them.
- Offered when: you had a large audience by floor 3. This class is one of the ways back from death.
- Tier 2: **Viral Archlich**.

**Wedding Band Bard** — *the cover band that never stops*
- Signature abilities: **Feedback**: a wave of sound that knocks enemies back. **Bridge**: a defensive song; you take less damage
  while you keep playing. **Crowd Pleaser**: party buffs that last as long as the song.
- Drawback: being hit hard stops the song and its buffs.
- Offered when: you used sound or performed for viewers.
- Tier 2: **Headliner of the Damned**.

**Mall Santa Warlord** — *who is naughty is decided by you*
- Signature abilities: **Naughty List**: mark an enemy; the whole party deals more damage to it. **Workshop Elves**: summon decoys.
  **Lap Gift**: hand an ally a random buff from your sack.
- Drawback: children-type NPCs follow you everywhere.
- Offered when: you gave items to others, or spent long on floor 1.
- Tier 2: **Yule Tyrant**.

## Plain classics

Not jokes: these are full classes whose strength is being reliable at everything their role needs, and whose
weakness is having no trick nobody saw coming.

**Regular Guy With a Sword** — *a fighter, exactly as advertised*
- Signature abilities: **Swing**: a strong, fast weapon attack with no cooldown to speak of. **Brace**: block a
  large share of the next hits. **Second Wind**: heal yourself and shrug off one harmful effect.
- Strength: the best weapon and armour skill growth of any class.
- Drawback: learns spells slowly and at a higher cost.
- Offered when: you fought mostly with weapons and kept your stats even.
- Tier 2: **Seasoned Guy With a Sword**.

**Wizard (Default Settings)** — *a mage with the factory presets*
- Signature abilities: **Firebolt**, **Frost Shield**, **Mend**. One of each school to start.
- Strength: can learn spells from every school at normal cost; no element is closed to them.
- Drawback: never the best at any one school.
- Offered when: you learned magic from several schools on floors 1 and 2.
- Tier 2: **Wizard (Custom Settings)**.
