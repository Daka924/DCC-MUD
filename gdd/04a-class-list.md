# Class list — Proposed

**Target (Decided): close to 150 classes.** This is the second draft of the first 33, rebuilt with the
framework in `04b-class-framework.md`. Numbers in *offered when* are placeholders until the skill list exists.

Changes from the first draft: the ice cream truck class is cut (vehicle); every class now has an archetype that
matches how it plays; no class is built on the audience; nothing measures talking or silence; offers ask for one
lean, not a whole floor; drawbacks all bite while you are alive; taglines rewritten.

## Bruiser

**Gym Bro Berserker** — *Gets bigger every rep and throws monsters like plates.*
- Hook: Meter. Every hit fills the Pump bar; a full bar makes you larger and stronger for a short time.
- Core **Curl**: a two-handed smash that fills the bar. Answer **Spot Me**: you cannot drop below 1 health for 3
  seconds. Big move **Max Out**: grab the nearest big monster and slam it into the crowd.
- Stats: Strength / Constitution. Skills: unarmed, heavy weapons, endurance.
- Drawback: Intelligence capped at 10; spells cost double.
- Offered when: 200 unarmed or heavy-weapon kills.
- Tier 2: **Olympian of the Iron**. Max Out can chain into a second throw.

**Repo Knight** — *Rips the buffs off monsters and wears them home.*
- Hook: Consume. Hits steal buffs from enemies onto you.
- Core **Repossess**: a heavy swing that takes one buff. Answer **Lien**: damage you take is spread out over 5
  seconds instead of landing at once. Big move **Foreclosure**: strip every buff in an area and keep the best one.
- Stats: Strength / Constitution. Skills: heavy weapons, armour.
- Drawback: you cannot receive buffs from allies; only stolen ones.
- Offered when: 100 kills on elite or buffed enemies.
- Tier 2: **Collections Crusader**. Stolen buffs last twice as long and can be passed to allies.

## Duelist

**Overnight Stocker Assassin** — *Strikes from the dark between the shelves and is gone before cleanup.*
- Hook: Combo. Attacks from behind after leaving stealth deal triple damage.
- Core **Box Cutter**: fast strikes, stronger from behind. Answer **Wet Floor**: drop a slick and vanish; pursuers
  slip. Big move **Inventory Night**: dash between up to five enemies, striking each.
- Stats: Dexterity / Strength. Skills: light blades, stealth.
- Drawback: low health; take 25% more damage from area attacks.
- Offered when: 75 kills from stealth.
- Tier 2: **Graveyard Shift Shade**. Inventory Night refunds its cooldown if it kills everything.

## Guardian

**Toll Booth Warden** — *Plants a booth in the road and makes every monster pay to pass.*
- Hook: Placed objects. You set down a booth and a line; monsters crossing the line are marked.
- Core **Exact Change**: monsters crossing your line take damage, more each time. Answer **Booth**: shut yourself in
  and block everything for 3 seconds. Big move **Fast Lane**: a lane where allies speed up and monsters crawl.
- Stats: Constitution / Strength. Skills: shields, armour.
- Drawback: 30% weaker while you are moving.
- Offered when: blocked 2,000 damage with defensive abilities.
- Tier 2: **Turnpike Authority**. Place two booths and a line between them.

**Bouncer Golem** — *A slab of stone muscle that decides who gets in.*
- Hook: Placed objects. Your rope barrier shapes the fight.
- Core **Not Tonight**: a shove that sends monsters flying. Answer **VIP**: an ally in your aura has half their
  damage moved to you. Big move **Velvet Rope**: a barrier monsters cannot cross for 8 seconds.
- Stats: Constitution / Strength. Skills: shields, unarmed.
- Drawback: you are slow, and cannot use stealth.
- Offered when: took 5,000 damage while an ally was within 5 tiles.
- Tier 2: **Door God**. Velvet Rope reflects what hits it.

**Personal Injury Paladin** — *Takes the hit, files the claim, collects in blood.*
- Hook: Meter. Damage taken fills a Settlement bar.
- Core **Objection**: a shield bash that interrupts and stuns. Answer **Whiplash**: for 4 seconds, all damage you
  take goes into the bar. Big move **Payout**: spend the bar as a burst of holy damage around you.
- Stats: Constitution / Charisma. Skills: shields, holy magic.
- Drawback: healing on you is halved while the bar is above half.
- Offered when: survived 20 times below 10% health.
- Tier 2: **Litigator Saint**. Payout also heals allies for a share of the damage.

**Customer Service Martyr** — *Smiles through every blow and sends the pain up the chain.*
- Hook: Link. You tie a monster to yourself; damage it does to you also hurts it.
- Core **Escalation**: link to a monster; each hit it lands on you stacks damage back on it. Answer **Hold Music**:
  everything around you slows for 5 seconds while you heal. Big move **Speak to the Manager**: summon a huge spirit
  that pummels the linked monster.
- Stats: Constitution / Charisma. Skills: endurance, curses.
- Drawback: armour does not reduce damage you take.
- Offered when: taken 3,000 damage without using a movement ability for the whole fight, 10 times.
- Tier 2: **Supervisor of Suffering**. Link two monsters at once.

## Caster

**Dirty Water Pyromancer** — *Hurls flaming relish and fries anything that comes close.*
- Hook: Terrain. Your fire leaves burning grease on the floor.
- Core **Relish Fire**: a cone of burning condiment. Answer **Umbrella Stand**: a heat shield that burns attackers.
  Big move **Grease Fire**: light every grease patch at once.
- Stats: Intelligence / Dexterity. Skills: fire magic.
- Drawback: on water or in rain, your fire does half damage.
- Offered when: 300 fire kills, or Fire Magic 5.
- Tier 2: **Food Truck Inferno Lord**. Grease patches spread toward enemies.

**Asphaltomancer** — *Tears the floor into potholes and paves monsters in boiling tar.*
- Hook: Terrain. You leave potholes and tar that change how monsters move.
- Core **Pothole**: the floor opens under a group of monsters. Answer **Fresh Blacktop**: a tar patch around you
  that roots and burns. Big move **Road Work Ahead**: a long line of exploding asphalt.
- Stats: Intelligence / Constitution. Skills: earth magic.
- Drawback: your abilities do nothing on water or ice.
- Offered when: 300 earth kills, or Earth Magic 5.
- Tier 2: **Turnpike Archmage**. Potholes swallow small monsters whole.

**Bee Mage** — *Opens a palm and lets the swarm out.*
- Hook: Stacks. Every bee sting adds venom; at 10 stacks the target swells and bursts.
- Core **Swarm**: a cloud of bees that chases the nearest monster. Answer **Hive Armour**: bees cover you, stinging
  anything that touches you. Big move **Queen**: summon a giant queen that releases swarms for 10 seconds.
- Stats: Intelligence / Dexterity. Skills: nature magic, poison.
- Drawback: fire damage hurts you 50% more and kills your swarms.
- Offered when: 200 poison kills, or adopted an insect pet.
- Tier 2: **Hive Archon**. Bursting targets release a new swarm.

**Podcast Host Seer** — *Blasts monsters with mic feedback and marks their weak spots for the party.*
- Hook: Stacks. Feedback hits reveal weak spots; the party deals more damage to revealed monsters.
- Core **Feedback**: a cone of sound damage. Answer **Ad Break**: a 2-second pulse that pushes everything away from
  you. Big move **Deep Dive**: reveal the map around you and mark every monster's weakness.
- Stats: Intelligence / Charisma. Skills: sound magic, mapping.
- Drawback: low health; sound magic cannot hurt deaf or soundproof monsters.
- Offered when: found 3 maps or 20 secret areas.
- Tier 2: **Network Prophet**. Deep Dive also shows the floor's hidden stairwells.

## Marksman

**Skee-Ball Sharpshooter** — *Rolls heavy balls that bounce off walls and through crowds.*
- Hook: Combo. Each wall bounce makes a ball hit harder.
- Core **Lane Roll**: a heavy ball that bounces up to three times. Answer **Ticket Spit**: a burst that knocks back
  everything around you. Big move **Hundred-Pointer**: a huge ball that bounces through a whole room.
- Stats: Dexterity / Strength. Skills: ranged weapons, aim.
- Drawback: poor in open areas with nothing to bounce off.
- Offered when: 150 ranged kills.
- Tier 2: **Boardwalk Champion**. Balls split on their final bounce.

## Summoner

**Funeral Director Necromancer** — *Raises the fresh dead and drives them like pallbearers.*
- Hook: Minions, made from corpses.
- Core **Open Casket**: raise a corpse to fight for 20 seconds. Answer **Embalm**: your skin turns to stone for 4
  seconds. Big move **Procession**: every corpse nearby rises at once.
- Stats: Intelligence / Constitution. Skills: death magic.
- Drawback: living NPCs charge you 50% more in shops.
- Offered when: looted 100 corpses, or 100 death magic kills.
- Tier 2: **Undertaker of the Deep**. Raised dead explode when they expire.

**Wedding Planner Summoner** — *Marches a bridal party of minions into battle on a strict schedule.*
- Hook: Combo. Your minions are stronger if you give orders in the set order.
- Core **Bridal Party**: summon four minions. Answer **Seating Chart**: swap places with one of them. Big move **First
  Dance**: all minions rush one target together.
- Stats: Charisma / Intelligence. Skills: summoning, leadership.
- Drawback: your minions vanish after 30 seconds without combat.
- Offered when: recruited 3 NPC companions.
- Tier 2: **Matrimonial Archon**. Adds a best man who taunts monsters away from you.

**Influencer Lich** — *Raises followers from the dead and hides its soul in a cracked phone.*
- Hook: Minions that grow in number as you kill.
- Core **Follow**: each kill adds a ghostly follower, up to 8. Answer **Phylactery**: once per floor, if your phone
  survives, you come back from death at it. Big move **Viral**: every follower explodes in a chain.
- Stats: Intelligence / Charisma. Skills: death magic, summoning.
- Drawback: the phone is a real item someone can break or steal. Holy damage hurts you double.
- Offered when: 500 total kills, or Death Magic 5.
- Tier 2: **Viral Archlich**. Followers copy one ability of the monster they came from.

**Mall Santa Warlord** — *Sends elves to swarm whoever made the naughty list.*
- Hook: Minions plus a mark.
- Core **Naughty List**: mark a monster; your elves always attack it first. Answer **Workshop Decoys**: three
  decoys that draw fire. Big move **Sleigh Bells**: elves pour in from every direction for 8 seconds.
- Stats: Charisma / Strength. Skills: summoning.
- Drawback: you are slow, and your elves are weak to fire.
- Offered when: gave 20 items to allies or companions.
- Tier 2: **Yule Tyrant**. The marked monster's allies turn on it.

## Handler

Pets follow the rules in `09-multiplayer.md`: everyone can adopt one pet they find. Handler classes add more pet
slots and give pets orders. Pets level up, can die, and count toward the party of 6.

**Dog Walker Packmaster** — *Walks into the fight with six leashes in one hand.*
- Hook: Pack. +2 pet slots (3 total). Every pet fights harder while another pet is within 3 tiles of it.
- Core **Sic 'Em**: every pet rushes the target you click. Answer **Heel**: pets snap back to you and take half the
  damage aimed at you for 4 seconds. Big move **Off Leash**: pets frenzy for 8 seconds, attacking twice as fast.
- Stats: Charisma / Dexterity. Skills: animal handling, whips.
- Drawback: your own attacks deal 30% less damage.
- Offered when: a pet dealt 2,000 damage, or Animal Handling 5.
- Tier 2: **Kennel Lord**. +1 pet slot, and pets share one buff you give any of them.

**Exotic Pet Shop Clerk** — *Nets whatever the dungeon throws at them and sells it a collar.*
- Hook: Tame. A monster below 25% health can be caught and becomes a pet, keeping one of its abilities.
- Core **Catch Net**: a net that roots a monster; if it is below 25% health, it is tamed. Answer **Feeding Time**: heal
  all pets and draw nearby monsters' attention to a thrown treat. Big move **Clearance Sale**: release a tamed pet
  as a one-time burst of its strongest ability, then it returns to its cage.
- Stats: Charisma / Dexterity. Skills: animal handling, traps.
- Drawback: +1 pet slot only (2 total); elites and bosses cannot be tamed until tier 2.
- Offered when: weakened 50 monsters below 25% health with a control ability, or adopted a pet.
- Tier 2: **Menagerie Baron**. Can tame elites, and a tamed elite keeps one of its affixes.

**Rooftop Pigeon Keeper** — *Whistles, and a flock of dive-bombing pigeons drops out of the rafters.*
- Hook: Pack. The flock fills one pet slot and has 12 birds; lost birds come back slowly between fights.
- Core **Release**: the flock dives at a target, each bird a small hit. Answer **Coop**: the flock wraps around you as
  a shield that breaks after 5 hits. Big move **Homing Run**: every bird dive-bombs in a line across the room.
- Stats: Dexterity / Charisma. Skills: animal handling, aim.
- Drawback: area attacks kill birds fast; a hit that covers the flock can halve it.
- Offered when: a pet survived 30 fights, or 150 ranged kills with a pet nearby.
- Tier 2: **Lord of the Rooftops**. The flock grows to 20, and birds carry the element of your last ability.

## Controller

**HOA Inquisitor** — *Stacks violations on monsters until the fine comes due.*
- Hook: Stacks. Three violations and the target is fined: big damage and a stun.
- Core **Violation Notice**: a bolt that adds one violation. Answer **Bylaws**: in a zone around you, monsters
  cannot use abilities for 3 seconds. Big move **Assessment**: fine everything in an area at once.
- Stats: Intelligence / Charisma. Skills: curses, holy magic.
- Drawback: faction reputation gains are halved.
- Offered when: 150 kills on enemies under a curse or debuff.
- Tier 2: **Grand Covenant Enforcer**. Fined monsters spread one violation to their neighbours.

**DMV Clerk Chronomancer** — *Makes monsters take a number and wait their turn to live.*
- Hook: Charge-up. The longer you hold, the longer the delay.
- Core **Take a Number**: freeze one monster in place for up to 5 seconds. Answer **Window Closed**: freeze yourself,
  untouchable, for 3 seconds. Big move **Expedited**: reset the cooldowns of every ally nearby.
- Stats: Intelligence / Dexterity. Skills: time magic.
- Drawback: your own cooldowns are 25% longer than anyone else's.
- Offered when: kept enemies slowed or frozen for a total of 30 minutes.
- Tier 2: **Bureau of Eternity**. Take a Number can hold three monsters at once.

**Crossing Guard Sentinel** — *Holds up a sign and the whole lane stops.*
- Hook: Placed objects. Everything you do works in straight lines.
- Core **STOP**: freeze every monster in a line for 2 seconds. Answer **Reflective Vest**: reflect the next projectile.
  Big move **Safe Crossing**: allies in a lane cannot be hit for 3 seconds.
- Stats: Constitution / Intelligence. Skills: shields, control magic.
- Drawback: your abilities only work in straight lines.
- Offered when: protected companions: an NPC ally survived 10 fights below 25% health.
- Tier 2: **Warden of the Crossroads**. Two lanes at once, in a cross.

**Mattress Salesman Dreamweaver** — *Lulls monsters to sleep on memory foam, then wakes them hard.*
- Hook: Combo. Hitting a sleeping monster deals triple damage and wakes it.
- Core **Going Out of Business**: put monsters in an area to sleep. Answer **Memory Foam**: absorb the next three hits
  completely. Big move **Mattress Firm**: a slab drops on a sleeping group.
- Stats: Intelligence / Constitution. Skills: sleep magic.
- Drawback: after each ability you act 20% slower for 2 seconds.
- Offered when: put 100 monsters to sleep or stunned 200.
- Tier 2: **Lord of the Showroom Floor**. Sleeping monsters heal your allies.

**Auctioneer Hexer** — *Curses a target once, twice, and on the third call it's sold.*
- Hook: Stacks. Three calls on one target and it is sold: weak targets die outright, strong ones take huge damage.
- Core **Going Once**: a curse bolt that adds one call. Answer **Sold!**: teleport a short way and leave a cursed
  double behind. Big move **Bidding War**: monsters in an area turn on each other for 5 seconds.
- Stats: Intelligence / Dexterity. Skills: curses.
- Drawback: switching to a new target before the third call wastes the stacks.
- Offered when: sold 100 items to merchants, or 100 curse kills.
- Tier 2: **Auction House Overlord**. Sold monsters drop extra loot.

## Healer

**Diner Oracle** — *Keeps the party alive on bottomless coffee, and throws the pot when that fails.*
- Hook: Placed objects. You set down a table where allies heal.
- Core **Bottomless Refill**: heal an ally over time; their next ability is free. Answer **Hot Coffee**: a cone of
  scalding coffee. Big move **Open All Night**: a booth that heals everyone sitting in it for 10 seconds.
- Stats: Charisma / Intelligence. Skills: healing, cooking.
- Drawback: your abilities cost double if you have not eaten in the last 10 minutes.
- Offered when: healed 3,000 health on others, or Cooking 5.
- Tier 2: **Short-Order Sibyl**. Allies healed by you see incoming attacks marked on the floor.

**Lifeguard Templar** — *Pulls allies out of the deep end and whistles monsters still.*
- Hook: Link. A rescue line ties you to an ally.
- Core **Rescue Tube**: yank an ally to your side and shield them. Answer **Riptide**: rush out of danger in a wave.
  Big move **Whistle**: stun everything in front of you and heal everyone behind you.
- Stats: Constitution / Charisma. Skills: healing, swimming.
- Drawback: 25% weaker more than 10 tiles from water.
- Offered when: Swimming 5, or saved an ally under 10% health 10 times.
- Tier 2: **Tidewarden**. Rescue Tube can pull two allies.

## Enchanter

**Tattoo Artist Runecarver** — *Inks runes into allies' skin and traps into the floor.*
- Hook: Placed objects. Runes go on allies as buffs, or on the floor as traps.
- Core **Fresh Ink**: give an ally a lasting buff rune. Answer **Flash Sheet**: a rune on the floor that bursts when
  stepped on. Big move **Full Sleeve**: every ally's rune flares at triple strength for 6 seconds.
- Stats: Charisma / Dexterity. Skills: runes, crafting.
- Drawback: each rune costs you 10% of your health to ink, and each ally can carry only one.
- Offered when: used 50 buff items or scrolls.
- Tier 2: **Master of the Living Ink**. Runes on allies move and attack nearby monsters.

**Wedding Band Bard** — *Plays the same four songs, and every one of them hits.*
- Hook: Stance. Four songs, one at a time, each a visible aura with its own buff.
- Core **Set List**: switch songs (speed, damage, defence, healing). Answer **Feedback Squeal**: knock back everything
  near you. Big move **Last Dance**: all four songs at once for 8 seconds.
- Stats: Charisma / Constitution. Skills: music, sound magic.
- Drawback: taking a big hit (over 20% of your health) stops the song for 3 seconds.
- Offered when: buffed allies for a total of 20 minutes.
- Tier 2: **Headliner of the Damned**. Songs also hurt monsters inside the aura.

**Union Steward Warlock** — *Calls a work stoppage on monsters and makes the party share the load.*
- Hook: Link. You tie the party together.
- Core **Grievance**: a curse that grows each time its target hits an ally. Answer **Solidarity**: the party shares
  one health pool for 4 seconds. Big move **Work Stoppage**: everything in an area freezes for 3 seconds.
- Stats: Charisma / Constitution. Skills: curses, leadership.
- Drawback: 30% weaker with no ally within 10 tiles.
- Offered when: spent 2 hours in a party with real players or companions.
- Tier 2: **Archon of the Brotherhood**. Solidarity also shares buffs.

## Trapper

**Boardwalk Carny Trickster** — *Sets rigged games on the floor, and every monster loses.*
- Hook: Placed objects. Booths that trap or fool monsters.
- Core **Ring Toss**: a trap that binds whatever walks into it. Answer **Funhouse Mirror**: three copies of you that
  draw attacks. Big move **Whack-a-Mole**: hammers burst out of the floor across a whole room.
- Stats: Dexterity / Intelligence. Skills: traps, tricks.
- Drawback: merchants will not buy from you.
- Offered when: 100 kills with traps or placed objects.
- Tier 2: **Ringmaster of the Last Pier**. Booths can be moved once placed.

**Fireworks Stand Operator** — *Smuggles crates across the state line and lights the fuse under everything.*
- Hook: Charge-up. Fuses burn longer for bigger blasts.
- Core **Roman Candle**: plant a launcher that fires at nearby monsters. Answer **Smoke Bomb**: vanish in coloured
  smoke. Big move **Grand Finale**: every launcher and crate you placed goes off at once.
- Stats: Dexterity / Intelligence. Skills: explosives, crafting.
- Drawback: fire damage to you can set off your own crates.
- Offered when: crafted 20 explosives, or Explosives 5.
- Tier 2: **Pyrotechnics Baron**. Launchers turn to track targets.

## Plain classes

The System AI sighs every time one of these is picked, and keeps reminding the player.

**Regular Guy With a Sword** — *A guy. With a sword.*
- Hook: none. That is the point.
- Core **Sword Attack**. Answer **Block**. Big move **Big Sword Attack**.
- Stats: Strength / Constitution. Skills: swords, armour.
- Strength: the best weapon and armour growth in the game, and nothing about it can go wrong.
- Drawback: no tricks. Every ability does exactly what it says.
- Offered when: fought mostly with a sword.
- Tier 2: **Slightly Better Guy With a Sword**.

**Wizard (Basic)** — *Magic. The regular kind.*
- Hook: none.
- Core **Magic Bolt**. Answer **Magic Shield**. Big move **Bigger Magic Bolt**.
- Stats: Intelligence / Constitution. Skills: any magic school at normal cost.
- Strength: can learn every school of magic at normal cost.
- Drawback: never the best at any one school.
- Offered when: learned spells from 3 schools.
- Tier 2: **Wizard (Premium)**.

## Totals

33 classes: Bruiser 2, Duelist 1, Guardian 4, Caster 4, Marksman 1, Summoner 4, Handler 3, Controller 5,
Healer 2, Enchanter 3, Trapper 2, Plain 2. Thin archetypes (Duelist, Marksman, Healer, Trapper) get filled first on the way
to 150.
